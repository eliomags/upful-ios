## Review Notes for App Store Team

### Important: Paper Trading - No Real Money Involved

Upful is a **paper (simulated) trading platform**. The following points are critical to understand:

1. **Virtual Currency Only**: All trading utilizes simulated virtual currency. No real money is ever transacted through the app. Users do not deposit, withdraw, or risk any actual funds.

2. **Educational Purpose**: Upful is designed as an educational and competitive platform for users to learn stock market trading, investment strategies, and financial concepts in a risk-free environment.

3. **No Financial Transactions**: The app does not process payments, credit card transactions, or financial transfers. In-app purchases are limited to optional subscription tiers for premium features only, not for purchasing trading capital.

4. **Real Market Data**: While the app displays real market data and prices from actual exchanges, all user trades are simulated. The app does not execute any real orders on any exchange.

5. **Subscription Model**: Optional premium subscription tiers are available for additional features (advanced screeners, enhanced analytics, priority competition placement). These are feature subscriptions only—they do not represent financial services or investments.

6. **Compliance**: This app does not require financial services licenses as it does not handle real money, conduct brokerage services, or provide personalized financial advice. It is an educational gaming platform with financial market simulation.

7. **Target Audience**: While designed primarily for adults and older teens interested in finance, the app is appropriate for users 4+ as it contains no inappropriate content. All market data is presented factually without promotional or misleading content.

8. **Data Privacy**: Market data is sourced from public financial data providers. User data is encrypted and handled according to our privacy policy.
