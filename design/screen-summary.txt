Upful iOS App - Figma Design Summary
======================================
Source: https://www.figma.com/design/iMceNmkVwyu8lsYQSZhbXK/upful-%7C-eliomags?node-id=0-1&t=uNggjbP4hKTwmFZB-1

Theme
-----
- Primary color: Teal/dark (#008080-ish)
- Backgrounds: White
- Navigation: Bottom tab bar with 5 icons (Home, Search, Heart/Favorites, Chart, Settings)
- Device frame: 375x812 (iPhone X/11/12 standard)


Screens
-------

1. Subscribe & Win (Step 1)
   - Subscription pitch at $6.99/week
   - Benefits listed: daily/weekly/monthly competitions, unlimited stock screeners, save unlimited stocks and screeners, grow with platform
   - "Subscribe" CTA button (teal)

2. Subscribe & Win (Step 2)
   - Username input field (prefilled: johndoe)
   - Email input field (prefilled: johndoe@gmail.com)
   - Purpose: PayPal prize payout info
   - "Submit" CTA button (teal)

3. Thank You
   - Confirmation after subscribing
   - Thumbs-up graphic
   - "See Leaderboard" CTA button (teal)

4. Welcome / $25,000 Virtual Money
   - User receives $25,000 virtual money
   - "New month starts with 25K virtual money"
   - Option to "Build New Portfolio" or "Keep Old Ratios"
   - "See Leaderboard" CTA button

5. Leaderboard
   - Tabs: Top / Selected / Best
   - Ranked list of users with avatars
   - Shows portfolio values (e.g., $30,035, $18,412, $16,832, etc.)
   - Percentage changes shown
